Lantronix Reports Fiscal First-Quarter 2027 Financial Results
· | Net Revenue Increased over 8% Year-over-Year to $32.4 Million
· | Embedded IoT Solutions Revenue Increased 37% Year-over-Yea
· | GAAP Net Loss Improved to $463 Thousand, or ($0.01) per Share
· | Non-GAAP Net Income Increased 50% to $2.2 Million, or $0.05 per Share
· | Company Reaffirms Expectation for Double-Digit Revenue Growth in Fiscal 2027
IRVINE, Calif., Nov. 9, 2026 — Lantronix Inc. (Nasdaq: LTRX), a global provider of Edge Intelligence and Industrial IoT solutions that power NDAA-compliant unmanned systems, critical infrastructure and resilient enterprise networks, today reported results for the fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Management Commentary
“We entered fiscal 2027 from a position of strength, delivering 8% year-over-year revenue growth, non-GAAP gross margins above 45%, and a 50% increase in year-over-year non-GAAP net income,” said Saleel Awsare, president and CEO of Lantronix. “Our Embedded IoT Solutions revenue grew 37% year-over-year, driven by continued momentum across unmanned systems. Importantly, we're translating that success into opportunities beyond drones and defense. Recent design wins in advanced surveying and smart security monitoring for jobsite safety demonstrate how we're leveraging our edge computing and computer vision expertise across a broader range of commercial and industrial applications.
“The strategic tuck-in acquisition of Nero Global Tracking has expanded our pro forma software and services mix above 10% of total revenue, supporting our strategy of building a more predictable, higher-margin revenue base. We are also seeing growing interest in our out-of-band management solutions from customers deploying AI and data center infrastructure. Together, these developments reinforce our confidence in delivering double-digit revenue growth in fiscal 2027.”

Q1 FY2027 and Recent Business Highlights
· | Added the Open-Q 8550CS SOM to the U.S. Department of War’s Blue UAS Framework, giving drone OEMs a pre-vetted compute module that can reduce component-level qualification effort for defense and government programs.
· | Introduced Spotter at AUSA 2026, a compact robotics platform with onboard AI, vision, sensing and flight control for drones and mobile robots, expected to be available in the fourth quarter of calendar 2026.
· | Secured two design wins for Lantronix’s edge compute solutions in commercial and industrial computer vision. Trimble Inc. selected Lantronix to power its advanced surveying equipment, which incorporates multiple cameras and laser technology, while a leading provider of jobsite security solutions chose Lantronix to enable smart security cameras with 360-degree jobsite intelligence.
· | Launched MediaTek-based system-on-modules, making Lantronix the only North American supplier of MediaTek-based SOMs. The NDAA/TAA-compliant modules deliver 10 TOPS of AI performance at less than 4 watts for high-volume commercial drone, industrial and robotics programs.
· | Welcomed Jason Lamb as an independent director. Lamb is a former U.S. Navy SEAL officer and defense technology executive with more than 20 years of experience across special operations, technology development and investing, providing valuable insight as the Company scales in unmanned systems.
· | Launched the FOX5 Edge-to-Cloud intelligent serial gateway, which pairs with Percepxion™ to give critical asset operators real-time monitoring and predictive maintenance without a truck roll.

About Lantronix
Lantronix Inc. (NASDAQ: LTRX) is a global leader in Edge Intelligence and Industrial IoT solutions that power NDAA-compliant unmanned systems, critical infrastructure and resilient enterprise networks. It delivers intelligent computing, secure connectivity and remote management for mission-critical applications enabling customers to optimize operations, enhance security and accelerate digital transformation. Its comprehensive portfolio of hardware, software and services powers applications ranging from mission-critical autonomous platforms and edge analytics for critical infrastructure to intelligent surveillance and secure network management. By bringing intelligence to the network edge, Lantronix helps organizations achieve efficiency, security and a competitive edge in today’s AI-driven world. For more information, visit the Lantronix website.

Lantronix believes that the presentation of non-GAAP financial information, when presented in conjunction with the corresponding GAAP measures, provides important supplemental information to management and investors regarding financial and business trends relating to the company’s financial condition and results of operations. Management uses the aforementioned non-GAAP measures to monitor and evaluate ongoing operating results and trends to gain an understanding of our comparative operating performance. The non-GAAP financial measures disclosed by the company should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP, and the financial results calculated in accordance with GAAP and reconciliations of the non-GAAP financial measures to the financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP should be carefully evaluated. The non-GAAP financial measures used by the company may be calculated differently from, and therefore may not be comparable to, similarly titled measures used by other companies. The company has provided reconciliations of the non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures.
Non-GAAP net income (loss) is calculated by adjusting GAAP net income (loss) to exclude (i) share-based compensation and the employer portion of withholding taxes on stock grants, (ii) depreciation and amortization, (iii) interest income (expense), (iv) other income (expense), (v) income tax provision (benefit), (vi) restructuring, severance and related charges, (vii) acquisition related costs, (viii) impairment of long-lived assets, (ix) amortization of purchased intangibles, (x) amortization of manufacturing profit in acquired inventory, (xi) fair value remeasurement of earnout consideration, and (xii) loss on extinguishment of debt.
Non-GAAP EPS is calculated by dividing non-GAAP net income by non-GAAP weighted-average shares outstanding (diluted). For purposes of calculating non-GAAP EPS, the calculation of GAAP weighted-average shares outstanding (diluted) is adjusted to exclude share-based compensation, which, for GAAP purposes, is treated as proceeds assumed to be used to repurchase shares under the GAAP treasury stock method.
Guidance for non-GAAP net income is provided without a reconciliation to the most directly comparable GAAP measure due to the inherent difficulty of forecasting the timing or amount of certain items excluded from the forward-looking non-GAAP measure. These items are not reasonably estimable on a forward-looking basis without unreasonable effort and could have a material impact on GAAP net income (loss).
