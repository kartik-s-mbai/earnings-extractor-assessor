Erie Indemnity Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Net Income per Diluted Share was $3.48 for the Quarter and $9.80 for the Nine Months of 2026
Erie, Pa., October 29, 2026 - Erie Indemnity Company (NASDAQ: ERIE) today announced financial results for the quarter and nine months ending September 30, 2026. Net income was $182.0 million, or $3.48 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $182.9 million, or $3.50 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Net income was $512.8 million, or $9.80 per diluted share, in the first nine months of 2026, compared to $496.0 million, or $9.48 per diluted share, in the first nine months of 2025.

3Q 2026 Highlights
Operating income before taxes decreased $1.5 million, or 0.7 percent, in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Management fee revenue - policy issuance and renewal services increased $21.7 million, or 2.6 percent, in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Management fee revenue - administrative services increased $0.9 million, or 4.7 percent, in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Cost of operations - policy issuance and renewal services
◦Commissions increased $24.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by an increase in agent incentive compensation and the growth in direct and affiliated assumed written premium.
◦Non-commission expense decreased $1.1 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. Personnel costs increased $1.6 million primarily due to higher healthcare costs. Sales and advertising increased $1.8 million primarily due to an increase in agent-related costs. Acquisition and underwriting support costs decreased $5.8 million primarily due to lower postage and underwriting report costs. Technology and infrastructure costs increased $6.9 million primarily due to higher costs associated with technology initiatives, including internal-use software impairments, depreciation and amortization expense, and cloud usage costs.
Administrative and other costs decreased $5.5 million primarily due to lower credit card processing fees.
Income from investments before taxes totaled $19.9 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $21.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net investment income was $25.4 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $21.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net realized and unrealized investment losses were $5.4 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to gains of $1.3 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Nine Months 2026 Highlights
Operating income before taxes increased $18.8 million, or 3.4 percent, in the first nine months of 2026 compared to the first nine months of 2025.
•Management fee revenue - policy issuance and renewal services increased $92.1 million, or 3.8 percent, in the first nine months of 2026 compared to the first nine months of 2025.
•Management fee revenue - administrative services increased $4.0 million, or 7.4 percent, in the first nine months of 2026 compared to the first nine months of 2025.
•Cost of operations - policy issuance and renewal services
◦Commissions increased $96.6 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to the first nine months of 2025, primarily driven by an increase in agent incentive compensation and the growth in direct and affiliated assumed written premium.
◦Non-commission expense decreased $20.7 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to the first nine months of 2025. Personnel costs increased $6.7 million primarily due to increased incentive compensation driven by stronger performance metrics, and higher base compensation. The increase is partially offset by bonuses awarded to all employees in 2025 in recognition of our 100th anniversary. Acquisition and underwriting support costs decreased $11.5 million primarily due to lower underwriting report and postage costs. Technology and infrastructure costs increased $7.4 million primarily due to higher depreciation and amortization expense from technology initiatives. Professional fees decreased $12.1 million primarily due to reduced use of third-party services related to technology initiatives. Administrative and other costs decreased $9.2 million primarily due to lower credit card processing fees and charitable contributions related to the transition of charitable giving through the Erie Insurance Foundation.
Income from investments before taxes totaled $64.5 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $60.7 million in the first nine months of 2025. Net investment income was $71.5 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $61.0 million in the first nine months of 2025. Net realized and unrealized losses were $5.7 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to gains of $2.3 million in the first nine months of 2025. Net impairment losses recognized in earnings were $1.3 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $2.6 million in the first nine months of 2025.

Erie Insurance Group
Erie Insurance Group, based in Erie, Pennsylvania, is the 10th largest homeowners insurer, 11th largest automobile insurer, and 9th largest commercial lines insurer in the United States based on direct premiums written, according to AM Best Company. Founded in 1925, Erie Insurance is a Fortune 500 company and the 16th largest property/casualty insurer in the United States based on net premiums written. Rated A (Excellent) by AM Best, ERIE has nearly seven million policies in force and operates in 12 states and the District of Columbia.
News releases and more information are available on ERIE's website at www.erieinsurance.com.
