Permianville Royalty Trust Announces Monthly Cash Distribution
HOUSTON, Texas—(BUSINESS WIRE)—November 18, 2026
Permianville Royalty Trust (NYSE: PVL, the “Trust”) today announced a cash distribution to the holders of its units of beneficial interest of $0.009500 per unit, payable on December 14, 2026, to unitholders of record on November 30, 2026. The net profits interest calculation represents reported oil production for the month of August 2026 and reported natural gas production during July 2026. The calculation includes accrued costs incurred in September 2026.

Recorded oil cash receipts from the oil and gas properties underlying the Trust (the “Underlying Properties”) totaled $2.5 million for the current month on realized wellhead prices of $77.96/Bbl, down $0.6 million from the prior month’s oil cash receipts.
Recorded natural gas cash receipts from the Underlying Properties totaled $2.1 million for the current month on realized wellhead prices of $2.87/Mcf, down $0.3 million from the prior month.
Total accrued operating expenses increased by $0.2 million from the prior month to $2.8 million, and capital expenditures increased by $1.6 million from the prior month to $3.2 million.
As previously disclosed, COERT Holdings 1 LLC (the “Sponsor”) has established a cash reserve for approved, future development expenses associated with ongoing Haynesville drilling and to account for the recent volatility in oil prices and the potential for increased capital expenditures by operators of the Underlying Properties. Given the increase in the current month’s reported capital spending, the Sponsor has notified the Trustee that it is releasing $1.7 million of the previously disclosed cash reserve of $2.0 million that it has established for approved, future development expenses on the Underlying Properties. The Sponsor will continue to hold the remaining cash reserve of $0.4 million to fund an expected increase in billed development expenses; however, if those expenses are ultimately delayed or are less than expected, or if the outlook changes, amounts reserved but unspent will be released as an incremental cash distribution in a future period.
As previously announced, in September 2026 the Sponsor closed on a partial sale of non-producing acreage for $0.3 million, free and clear of the Trust’s net profits interest. The portion of the sale proceeds attributable to the Trust’s net profits interests, after expenses, is included in the November 2026 distribution to unitholders. The sale also includes a future capital expenditure carry from the buyer on the retained acreage to the Underlying Properties.
About Permianville Royalty Trust
Permianville Royalty Trust is a Delaware statutory trust formed to own a net profits interest representing the right to receive 80% of the net profits from the sale of oil and natural gas production from certain, predominantly non-operated, oil and gas properties in the states of Texas, Louisiana and New Mexico. As described in the Trust’s filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”), the amount of the periodic distributions is expected to fluctuate, depending on the proceeds received by the Trust as a result of actual production volumes, oil and gas prices, the amount and timing of capital expenditures, and the Trust’s administrative expenses, among other factors. Future distributions are expected to be made on a monthly basis. For additional information on the Trust, please visit www.permianvilleroyaltytrust.com.
