CytoSorbents Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results, Achieves Operating Cash Flow Breakeven, and Provides Business and Regulatory Update

Delivering on the First of Four Distinct Value Drivers

PRINCETON, N.J., November 12, 2026 — CytoSorbents Corporation (NASDAQ: CTSO), a leader in the treatment of life-threatening conditions in the intensive care unit and cardiac surgery using blood purification, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, recent business highlights, and provides a regulatory update.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 •
Revenue was $9.7 million, an increase of 3% compared to $9.5 million in Q3 2025. Growth was led by a 16% increase in distributor and strategic partner sales.
 

 •
Gross margin improved to 75% in the quarter, up from 73% in Q2 2026 and 70% a year ago.
 

 •
Total operating expenses decreased 11% to $8.5 million from $9.5 million in Q3 2025. Excluding $0.25 million of restructuring charges, operating expenses decreased 13%.
 

 •
Operating loss improved by 59% to $1.2 million versus $2.9 million in Q3 2025.
 

 •
Net loss improved by 42% to $1.8 million or $0.58 per share, compared to a net loss of $3.2 million or $1.01 per share in Q3 2025. All per share amounts reflect the 1-for-20 reverse stock split effected on September 8, 2026.
 

 •
Adjusted net loss, which excludes the impact of non-cash changes in foreign currency transactions, non-cash stock compensation, and restructuring charges, improved by 42% to $1.5 million or $0.48 per share, compared to an adjusted net loss of $2.6 million or $0.83 per share in Q3 2025.
 

 •
Adjusted EBITDA loss, which excludes interest payments, depreciation, taxes, and amortization, as well as the impact of non-cash changes in foreign currency transactions, non-cash stock compensation, and restructuring charges, improved by 86% to $0.3 million compared to a loss of $2.0 million in Q3 2025.
 

 •
Total cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash was approximately $5.7 million on September 30, 2026, compared to $5.9 million at June 30, 2026.
 

 •
Importantly, the Company generated positive operating cash flow of approximately $43,000 in the quarter, excluding approximately $251,000 in restructuring-related payments, achieving its objective of operating cash flow breakeven in the second half of 2026. Total cash burn in the quarter, inclusive of restructuring-related payments, was approximately $200,000.

Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Results

 •
Revenue was $28.2 million compared to $27.8 million in the first nine months of 2025.
 

 •
Gross margin improved to 73% compared to 71% in the prior year period.
 

 •
Total operating expenses decreased 9% to $27.4 million, and operating loss improved by 34% to $6.9 million from $10.4 million.
 

 •
Net loss was $11.4 million or $3.62 per share, compared to $2.7 million or $0.87 per share in the prior year period, with the change due primarily to a $1.9 million non-cash foreign currency loss in 2026 versus a $9.1 million non-cash foreign currency gain in 2025.
 

 •
Adjusted EBITDA loss improved by 45% to $4.0 million compared to a loss of $7.3 million in the prior year period.

“The third quarter marked an important financial milestone for CytoSorbents as we achieved our goal of operating cash flow breakeven, excluding restructuring-related payments,” stated Dr. Phillip Chan, Chief Executive Officer of CytoSorbents.  “This achievement reflects a year of disciplined execution and meaningful improvements in our underlying business.  Product gross margin expanded to 75%, operating expenses declined 11%, operating loss improved 59%, and notably, adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed 86% to approximately $0.3 million.  These results demonstrate the impact of our more efficient cost structure and position us to benefit from improved operating leverage as revenue grows.  Importantly, every dollar of operating cash burn we eliminate helps reduce our future financing needs and positions us to capture more of the potential value of future growth for our  shareholders.

Progress on Our Four Distinct Value Drivers
“We continue to advance four distinct value drivers, each with the potential to create meaningful shareholder value.  These include achieving sustainable operating cash flow breakeven, restoring profitable growth in our core CytoSorb business, opening the U.S. market through DrugSorb-ATR, and unlocking the strategic value of HemoDefend-BGA.  With our first milestone achieved, we are focused on sustaining this progress while advancing the remaining three opportunities.

First Value Driver: Achieving Operating Cash Flow Breakeven — Achieved in Q3 2026
“In the third quarter, we generated approximately $43,000 in positive operating cash flow, excluding $251,000 in restructuring-related payments, compared with operating cash burn of approximately $200,000 in Q2 2026 on the same basis and $2.7 million in Q3 2025.

“This improvement reflects a broad-based effort to optimize our operations.  Manufacturing efficiencies and improved sourcing helped expand product gross margin to 75%, while a 24% reduction in headcount since September 2025 and disciplined expense management significantly lowered our cost structure.  We also improved working capital management, reducing inventory from $5.3 million at year-end 2025 to $3.2 million at September 30, 2026.

“Our focus now turns to sustaining operating cash flow at or near breakeven while selectively investing to accelerate sales growth, which has historically been constrained by limited resources. With a significantly leaner cost structure and healthy gross margins, we believe we are now better positioned to capitalize on growth opportunities and generate meaningful operating leverage as revenues increase. Although quarterly cash flow may fluctuate with the timing of revenue, collections, and working capital, our goal is to maintain financial discipline while translating incremental sales growth into improved profitability and long-term shareholder value.”

Second Value Driver: Returning CytoSorb to Sustainable, Profitable Growth
“Our second value driver is restoring consistent, profitable growth in our core CytoSorb franchise.  Encouragingly, distributor and strategic partner sales grew 18% year-over-year and 10% sequentially in the third quarter, while direct sales outside Germany increased 4% year-over-year.  Together, these markets accounted for 76% of quarterly sales, demonstrating continued momentum across the majority of our business.

“These gains were offset by a 20% year-over-year sales decline in Germany, which represented 24% of quarterly sales, primarily reflecting a smaller, more focused sales force and reduced territory coverage following our commercial restructuring.  Restoring growth in Germany is a top priority and an important opportunity to accelerate overall revenue growth.  To address this, we have already added two sales representatives, expect another next month, and add two more through early 2027.

We also recently welcomed Alex Tiefenbacher as Senior Vice President of Direct Sales, bringing extensive acute and critical care medical device sales leadership experience from C.R. Bard, Becton Dickinson, GE Healthcare, and Baxter.  Alex will lead our direct sales organization in Germany and internationally, working closely with our sales leaders in those two verticals, with an initial focus on revitalizing the German market and building a scalable platform for sustained growth.  We believe these targeted investments in commercial leadership and sales capacity will strengthen execution, expand territory coverage, deepen customer relationships, and position us to return to growth as new representatives become productive.

“Meanwhile, our 'Right Patient, at the Right Time, with the Right Dose' commercial strategy continues to gain traction, supported by our PuriFi® pump platform and HotSwap® technology, which simplify treatment delivery and enable earlier, more consistent CytoSorb use.  We are also

strengthening our clinical evidence base, including new data presented at the European Association for Cardio-Thoracic Surgery (EACTS) Annual Meeting highlighting applications in blood thinner removal, post-operative shock, VA-ECMO support, and heart transplantation, as well as findings from the international COSMOS critical care registry presented at the European Society of Intensive Care Medicine (ESICM) LIVES 2026 congress addressing sepsis, liver support, and VV-ECMO..

“We believe a combination of renewed growth in Germany, sustained international momentum, an expanding clinical evidence base, and a leaner cost structure positions our core CytoSorb franchise to accelerate revenue growth, leverage our attractive gross margins, and translate increasing sales into meaningful improvements in profitability and cash flow.

Third Value Driver: Opening the U.S.  Market Through DrugSorb-ATR
“Our third value driver is to obtain FDA marketing authorization for DrugSorb-ATR, potentially opening a second major engine for growth.  DrugSorb-ATR is designed to remove commonly prescribed blood thinners during cardiopulmonary bypass in patients requiring urgent cardiac surgery, with the goal of reducing serious and life-threatening bleeding complications.  Subject to regulatory authorization, we estimate an initial addressable market of approximately $300 million in the United States and Canada, that we estimate could expand to $1-2 billion with label expansion to other blood thinners and additional surgical indications.

“During the quarter, we made meaningful progress on our new intended De Novo submission to FDA on DrugSorb-ATR.  At the European Society of Cardiology congress this past August, an independent patient-matched analysis evaluated 587 patients undergoing urgent coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) surgery following recent exposure to Brilinta® (ticagrelor).  The analysis demonstrated a statistically significant 37% relative reduction in severe BARC-4 bleeding with intraoperative CytoSorb use (21.8% versus 34.5% control; p=0.002), resulting in a clinically impactful NNT (Number Needed to Treat) of approximately eight patients to prevent one severe bleeding event.  Significant reductions in chest tube drainage and red blood cell transfusions were also observed, with no serious device-related adverse events.

“This analysis of real-world data is particularly important because it was previously proposed by the FDA during the appeal of our original De Novo submission and is expected to be a key component of our planned new De Novo submission in early 2027.  We expect this additional evidence to increase approximately fivefold the number of patients treated with our technology represented in the submission, to approximately 250 patients.  Meanwhile, based on a constructive FDA pre-submission meeting in August, we are working to finalize an animal study protocol with the FDA to collect additional mechanistic data. Taken together with STAR-T data, and previously submitted safety data, we believe this real-world analysis and mechanistic data

will provide a substantially expanded body of evidence supporting the clinical benefit of DrugSorb-ATR for FDA review.

“In parallel, in a separate FDA pre-submission meeting, we advanced discussions with the Agency regarding the data requirements of a separate De Novo submission expected next year for the removal of direct oral anticoagulants, including Eliquis® (apixaban) and Xarelto® (rivaroxaban), during urgent cardiac surgery, which could significantly expand the addressable U.S. market for our technology given the widespread use of these medications.

“Importantly, the Brilinta® and direct oral anticoagulant programs offer two distinct potential pathways to U.S. commercialization, each supported by its own clinical evidence and regulatory strategy.

Fourth Value Driver: Unlocking the Strategic Value of HemoDefend-BGA
“Our fourth value driver is realizing the strategic value of HemoDefend-BGA, a differentiated blood purification technology that we believe is not fully reflected in our current valuation.

“Developed with approximately $16 million in prior non-dilutive U.S. government funding, HemoDefend-BGA is designed to remove anti-A and anti-B antibodies from plasma and platelet products, with the goal of enabling universal blood products that can be transfused regardless of recipient blood type - without electricity, capital equipment, or complex processing.

“The potential implications are substantial.  Rather than maintaining separate plasma and platelet inventories for four blood types, hospitals could potentially simplify inventory management to just one universal plasma and one universal platelet product, reducing logistical complexity, waste, and costs.  Longer term, the technology could enable first responders, ambulances, and military units to carry freeze-dried universal plasma that requires no refrigeration or blood typing, allowing faster treatment of life-threatening hemorrhage in emergency and battlefield settings.

“Following helpful FDA feedback in July regarding our anticipated clinical development pathway, we are evaluating opportunities to advance and realize the value of HemoDefend-BGA through additional non-dilutive government funding, commercial partnerships, licensing arrangements, and other strategic alternatives.  We believe this asset offers opportunities to create shareholder value without necessarily requiring substantial investment from our core operations.

Concluding Remarks
Dr.  Chan concluded, “With operating cash flow breakeven achieved and a more efficient cost structure in place, CytoSorbents is entering its next phase from a stronger operational position.  Our priorities are clear: sustaining financial discipline, restoring profitable growth in our core

CytoSorb franchise, advancing DrugSorb-ATR toward U.S. commercialization, and unlocking the strategic value of HemoDefend-BGA.

“These four distinct value drivers provide multiple potential pathways to shareholder value creation, reducing our dependence on any single commercial, regulatory, or strategic outcome.  With improving fundamentals, meaningful near-term catalysts, and substantial longer-term market opportunities, we believe CytoSorbents is positioned to become a stronger, more profitable, and more diversified medical technology company.”

About CytoSorbents Corporation (NASDAQ: CTSO)
CytoSorbents Corporation is a leader in the treatment of life-threatening conditions in the intensive care unit and cardiac surgery through blood purification. CytoSorbents’ proprietary blood purification technologies are based on biocompatible, highly porous polymer beads that can actively remove toxic substances from blood and other bodily fluids by pore capture and surface adsorption. Cartridges filled with these beads can be used with standard blood pumps already in the hospital (e.g. dialysis, continuous renal replacement therapy or CRRT, extracorporeal membrane oxygenation or ECMO, and heart-lung machines), where blood is repeatedly recirculated outside the body, through our cartridges where toxic substances are removed, and then back into the body. CytoSorbents’ technologies are used in a number of broad applications. Specifically, two important applications are 1) the removal of blood thinners during and after cardiothoracic surgery to reduce the risk of severe bleeding, and 2) the removal of inflammatory agents and toxins in common critical illnesses that can lead to massive inflammation, organ failure and patient death. The breadth of these critical illnesses includes, for example, sepsis, burn injury, trauma, lung injury, liver failure, cytokine release syndrome, and pancreatitis as well as the removal of liver toxins that accumulate in acute liver dysfunction or failure, and the removal of myoglobin in severe rhabdomyolysis that can otherwise lead to renal failure. In these diseases, the risk of death can be extremely high, and there are few, if any, effective treatments.

CytoSorbents’ lead product, CytoSorb®, is approved in the European Union and distributed in over 70 countries worldwide, with more than 300,000 devices used cumulatively to date. CytoSorb was originally launched in the European Union under CE mark as the first cytokine adsorber. Additional CE mark extensions were granted for bilirubin and myoglobin removal in clinical conditions such as liver disease and trauma, respectively, and for ticagrelor and rivaroxaban removal in cardiothoracic surgery procedures. CytoSorb has also received FDA Emergency Use Authorization in the United States for use in adult critically ill COVID-19 patients with impending or confirmed respiratory failure. CytoSorb is not yet approved or cleared in the United States.

In the U.S. and Canada, CytoSorbents is developing the DrugSorb™-ATR antithrombotic removal system, an investigational device based on an equivalent polymer technology to CytoSorb, to

reduce the severity of perioperative bleeding in high-risk surgery due to blood thinning drugs. It has received two FDA Breakthrough Device Designations: one for the removal of ticagrelor and another for the removal of the direct oral anticoagulants (DOAC) apixaban and rivaroxaban in a cardiopulmonary bypass circuit during urgent cardiothoracic surgery. The Company is actively pursuing regulatory approval of DrugSorb-ATR with the U.S. FDA and will pursue regulatory approval with Health Canada with better visibility from the FDA. DrugSorb-ATR is not yet granted or approved in either the U.S. or Canada.

The Company has numerous marketed products and products under development based upon this unique blood purification technology protected by many issued U.S. and international patents and registered trademarks, and multiple patent applications pending, including ECOS-300CY®, CytoSorb-XL™, HemoDefend-RBC™, HemoDefend-BGA™, VetResQ®, K+ontrol™, DrugSorb™, ContrastSorb, PuriFi®, HotSwap®, and others.
