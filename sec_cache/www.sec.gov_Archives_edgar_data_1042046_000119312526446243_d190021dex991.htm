CINCINNATI – November 3, 2026 – American Financial Group, Inc. (NYSE: AFG) today reported 2026 third quarter net earnings of $282 million ($3.40 per share) compared to $215 million ($2.58 per share) in the 2025 third quarter. Net earnings included net after-tax non-core items that reduced net income by $47 million ($0.57 per share loss) and $9 million ($0.11 per share loss) in the 2026 and 2025 third quarters, respectively. Annualized return on equity was 22.3% and 18.2% for the third quarters of 2026 and 2025, respectively, and is calculated excluding accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI). Other details may be found in the table on the following page.

Core net operating earnings were $329 million ($3.97 per share) for the 2026 third quarter, compared to $224 million ($2.69 per share) in the 2025 third quarter, reflecting higher year-over-year net investment income. Additional details for the 2026 and 2025 third quarters may be found in the table below. Core net operating earnings for the third quarters of 2026 and 2025 generated annualized returns on equity of 26.0% and 19.0%, respectively, which is calculated excluding AOCI.

Specialty Property and Casualty Insurance Operations

The Specialty P&C insurance operations generated a 93.8% combined ratio in the third quarter of 2026, 0.8 points higher than the 93.0% reported in the comparable prior year period. Third quarter 2026 results included 1.0 point related to catastrophe losses, compared to 1.2 points in the 2025 third quarter. Third quarter 2026 results benefited from 1.5 points of favorable prior year reserve development, compared to 1.2 points in the third quarter of 2025.

Underwriting profit was $133 million for the 2026 third quarter compared to $139 million for the 2025 third quarter. Higher year-over-year underwriting profit in our Property and Transportation and Specialty Casualty Groups was more than offset by lower underwriting profit in our Specialty Financial Group.

Third quarter 2026 gross and net written premiums increased 8% and 9%, respectively, when compared to the third quarter of 2025. We continued to achieve premium growth across our portfolio as a result of a combination of new business opportunities, a favorable renewal rate environment and strong policy retention, all while maintaining discipline and focusing on underwriting profitability.

Average renewal pricing across our P&C Group, excluding workers’ compensation, was up approximately 6% for the quarter, about a point higher than in the previous quarter. Average renewal rates, including workers’ compensation, were up approximately 5% overall, also about a point higher than in the second quarter. We believe we are achieving overall renewal rate increases that enable us to meet or exceed targeted returns.

The Property and Transportation Group reported 2026 third quarter underwriting profit of $68 million, compared to $55 million in the third quarter of 2025, reflecting higher underwriting profit in our agricultural and transportation businesses. Catastrophe losses in this group were $4 million (0.4 points on the combined ratio) in both of the third quarters of 2026 and 2025. Overall, the businesses in the Property and Transportation Group achieved a 93.4% calendar year combined ratio in the third quarter, an improvement of 0.7 points from the comparable period in 2025.

Third quarter 2026 gross and net written premiums in this group were 12% and 14% higher, respectively, than the comparable prior year. The increase is primarily attributable to growth in certain crop insurance products, along with new business opportunities and a favorable rate environment in several of our transportation businesses. Overall renewal rates in this group increased approximately 7% in the third quarter of 2026, slightly below the pricing increase achieved in this group for the second quarter of 2026.

The Specialty Casualty Group reported 2026 third quarter underwriting profit of $42 million, compared to $33 million in the third quarter of 2025. Higher profitability in our excess and surplus and targeted markets businesses was partially offset by lower levels of favorable prior year development in our workers’ compensation businesses. Underwriting profitability in our workers’ compensation businesses overall continues to be very strong. The businesses in the Specialty Casualty Group achieved a solid 94.9% calendar year combined ratio in the third quarter of 2026, an improvement of 0.9 points from the 95.8% reported in the comparable period in 2025.

 

Page 3

Third quarter 2026 gross written premiums in this group increased less than 1%, and net written premiums increased 2% when compared to the same prior year period. New business opportunities, strong policy retention and higher rates contributed to growth in several of our Specialty Casualty businesses, which was partially offset by non-renewals of under-performing accounts in certain targeted markets businesses. Excluding workers’ compensation, renewal pricing for this group was up approximately 6% in the third quarter; pricing including workers’ compensation was up about 4%. Both measures are an improvement of 2 points over pricing achieved in the second quarter.

The Specialty Financial Group reported an underwriting profit of $23 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $51 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease reflects an isolated large loss in our European operations and lower overall levels of favorable prior year development. Catastrophe losses for this group were $16 million (5.2 points on the combined ratio) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $11 million (4.1 points) in the prior year quarter. This group reported a combined ratio of 92.5% for the third quarter of 2026, 11.4 points higher than the exceptionally strong results reported in the prior year period.

Third quarter 2026 gross and net written premiums in this group both increased approximately 14% when compared to the prior year period, primarily due to growth across our lender services businesses. Renewal pricing in this group was up approximately 4% in the third quarter.

Beyond strong P&C operating earnings, I am very pleased to see our teams continue to execute on opportunities to grow while achieving favorable renewal rate increases, which helps to ensure that we are meeting or exceeding overall targeted returns. I like our positioning and momentum as we enter the fourth quarter.”

Investments

P&C Net Investment Income – Net investment income in our property and casualty insurance operations for the three months ended September 30, 2026 established a new quarterly record for AFG, increasing 41% year-over-year to $289 million. The increase was primarily attributable to income from the sale of Charleston Harbor Resort & Marina. Consistent with what we had previously communicated, the August 2026 sale of Charleston Harbor Resort & Marina generated $126 million in pretax net investment income. Since the P&C companies and AFG parent each owned half of the property, the earnings are split evenly between the operating results of the P&C Insurance Segment and holding company investment income. Property and casualty net investment income – excluding the income from the sale of Charleston Harbor Resort & Marina – increased 10% when compared to the 2025 third quarter.

The annualized return on alternative investments was approximately 6.7% for the 2026 third quarter compared to 6.2% for the prior year quarter. Earnings from alternative investments may vary from quarter to quarter based on the reported results of the underlying investments and generally are reported on a quarter lag. Longer term, we continue to remain optimistic regarding the prospects of attractive returns from our alternative investment portfolio, with an expectation of annual returns averaging 10% or better.

Non-Core Net Realized Gains (Losses) – AFG recorded third quarter 2026 net realized losses of $28 million ($0.34 per share loss) after tax, which included $6 million ($0.07 per share loss) in after-tax net losses to adjust equity securities that the Company continued to own at September 30, 2026, to fair value. AFG recorded net realized gains of $11 million ($0.13 per share) in the comparable 2025 period.

After-tax unrealized losses related to fixed maturities were $400 million at September 30, 2026. A&E Reserves

As in prior years, during the third quarter, AFG conducted an in-depth internal review of its asbestos and environmental (A&E) exposures relating to the run-off operations of its P&C Group. During the 2026 review, no new trends were identified, and recent claims activity was generally consistent with our expectations resulting from our in-depth reviews in the prior five years, and our most recent external study in 2020. As a result, and consistent with the internal review in the third quarter of 2025, the 2026 review resulted in no net change to the P&C Group’s A&E reserves.

At September 30, 2026, the P&C Group’s insurance reserves include A&E reserves of $332 million, net of reinsurance recoverables. At September 30, 2026, the property and casualty insurance segment’s three-year survival ratios were 18.8 times paid losses for asbestos reserves, 37.7 times paid losses for environmental reserves and 24.0 times paid losses for total A&E reserves. These ratios compare favorably with industry data compiled by S&P Global Market Intelligence as of December 31, 2025, which indicate that industry survival ratios were 7.8 times paid losses for asbestos, 8.3 times paid losses for environmental, and 8.0 times paid losses for total A&E reserves.

The 2026 in-depth comprehensive review also included reserves for asbestos and environmental exposures of our former railroad and manufacturing operations. As a result of the review, AFG recorded a special non-core A&E charge to increase its liabilities for environmental exposures by $24 million ($19 million after-tax), due primarily to increases in the anticipated scope and costs of investigation and remediation at a limited number of sites.

About American Financial Group, Inc.

American Financial Group is an insurance holding company, based in Cincinnati, Ohio. Through the operations of Great American Insurance Group, AFG is engaged primarily in property and casualty insurance, focusing on specialized commercial products for businesses. Great American Insurance Group’s roots go back to 1872 with the founding of its flagship company, Great American Insurance Company.
