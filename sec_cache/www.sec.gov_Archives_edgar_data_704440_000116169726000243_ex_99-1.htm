KORU Medical Systems Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results Including Second Consecutive Quarter of Positive Net Income and Adjusted EBITDA

 

 

MAHWAH, NJ – November 10, 2026 – KORU Medical Systems, Inc. (NASDAQ: KRMD) (“KORU Medical” or the “Company”), a leading medical technology company focused on the development, manufacturing, and commercialization of innovative and patient-centric large volume subcutaneous infusion solutions, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights

 

● Net revenues grew 11% over the prior year period to $11.6 million; year to date net revenues grew 17% to $35.4 million
   
● Gross profit grew 20% over the prior year period to $7.5 million, driven by gross margin improvement from 60.2% to 65.2%
   
● Net income improved by $1.0 million to $0.2 million compared to a net loss of $0.8 million in prior year period
   
● Adjusted EBITDA increased to $1.3 million from $0.1 million in the prior year period
   
● Signed two new sales and distribution agreements with partners in Europe and Africa, expanding our geographic footprint internationally
   
● Revising full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $48.0 - $48.5 million, representing year-over-year growth of 17% - 18%, compared to the previous guidance range of $47.5 - $48.5 million.

 

“Total Revenue of $11.6 million represents another quarter of consistent above-market growth for KORU, driven by our recurring patient base,” said Adam Kalbermatten, President and CEO of KORU Medical. “We also delivered positive net income and adjusted EBITDA for the second consecutive quarter, demonstrating the scalability of our business model. As anticipated, our year-over-year revenue growth moderated in the third quarter compared to the first half of 2026, reflecting in part the timing of our entry into a competitive non-tender market in Europe. With that market launch now behind us, we anticipate revenue growth to reaccelerate in the fourth quarter for a strong finish to the year. We are encouraged by our geographic expansion, specialty pharmacy partnerships, and pipeline advancements and plan to continue investing resources in our long-term growth strategy as we move forward.”

Total net revenues increased $1.2 million, or 11.2%, to $11.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $10.4 million in the prior year period.

 

Domestic core revenues were $8.1 million, an increase of $1.9 million, or 31.6%, compared to the prior-year period. Approximately $1.2 million of the increase reflected lower orders from a U.S. distributor in the third quarter of 2025, when the distributor purchased a portion of its KORU products through one of our international distributors and reduced its inventory levels. The remaining increase was primarily driven by higher pump and consumable volumes, new patient starts, and market share gains within existing accounts.

 

 

International core revenues were $3.1 million, a decrease of 16.5% compared to the prior-year period. The decrease primarily reflected a higher prior-year comparison base, which included shipments to a distributor in a strategic market in anticipation of demand associated with conversions from vials to prefilled products, as well as purchases by an international distributor for subsequent resale in the U.S. The decrease was partially offset by growth in new patient starts within existing international markets.

 

As discussed above, purchases through the international distributor for resale in the U.S. affected the geographic mix of Core revenues in the prior-year period and, consequently, the year-over-year comparisons of Domestic and International Core revenues.

 

Pharma services and clinical trials net revenues were $0.4 million, a decrease of 27.4% compared to the prior-year period, primarily reflecting lower purchases of products for use in a customer's clinical trial program.

 

Gross profit increased $1.3 million, or 20.5%, to $7.5 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $6.3 million in the prior year period, primarily driven by gross margin and volume growth. Gross margin increased to 65.2% for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to 60.2% in the prior year period, primarily driven by lower manufacturing costs, higher than average selling prices from our geographical customer mix and lower tariff costs driven by tariff related rebates.

 

Total operating expenses increased $0.2 million, or 3.2%, to $7.4 million for the third quarter of 2026 primarily driven by an increase of $0.3 million in research and development. The increase in research and development expenses was primarily due to higher salary and stock compensation related to headcount additions, including employees added in connection with our technology asset acquisition.

 

Net income increased $1.0 million to $0.2 million or $0.00 per diluted share for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a net loss of ($0.8) million, or ($0.02) per diluted share, for the prior year period. Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter was $1.3 million, or $0.02 per adjusted diluted share versus $0.1 million or $0.00 per adjusted diluted share in the prior year period. A reconciliation of adjusted EBITDA to the most directly comparable GAAP measures is provided at the end of this press release.

 

Cash and cash equivalents were $7.4 million as of September 30, 2026, reflecting cash usage of $0.9 million in the third quarter of 2026.

 

Updated 2026 Guidance

 

● Revising full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $48.0 - $48.5 million, representing year-over-year growth of 17% - 18%, compared to the previous guidance range of $47.5 - $48.5 million.
   
● Revising full year 2026 gross margin to be between 63 - 64%, from prior range of 62 – 64%
   
● Reiterating positive adjusted EBITDA for the full year
   
● Raising year-end cash balance to be greater than $8.0 million, from prior guidance of greater than $7.5 million

About KORU Medical Systems

 

KORU Medical Systems develops, manufactures, and commercializes innovative and patient-centric large volume subcutaneous infusion solutions that improve quality of life for patients around the world. The Freedom Syringe Infusion System (the “Freedom System”) currently includes the Freedom60® and FreedomEDGE® Syringe Infusion Drivers, Precision Flow Rate Tubing™ and HigH-Flo Subcutaneous Safety Needle Sets™. The Freedom System, which received its first FDA clearance in 1994, is used for self-administration in the home by the patient and/or delivery in an ambulatory infusion center by a healthcare professional. Through its Pharma Service and Clinical Trials business, KORU Medical provides products for use by biopharmaceutical companies in feasibility/clinical trials during the drug development process and, as needed, is capable of customizing the Freedom System for clinical and commercial use across multiple drug categories. For more information, please visit www.korumedical.com.
