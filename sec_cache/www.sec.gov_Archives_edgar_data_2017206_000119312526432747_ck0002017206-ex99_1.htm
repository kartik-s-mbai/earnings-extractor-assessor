Millrose Properties Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Record Third Quarter Net Income of $130 Million, or $0.78 Per Share

Third Quarter AFFO of $0.80 Per Share, the Sixth Consecutive Quarterly AFFO Increase; Raises Fourth Quarter AFFO Outlook to $0.82 to $0.84 Per Share

Board of Directors Authorized a New $250 Million Share Repurchase Program

Expanded Counterparty Base to 22 Homebuilder and Developer Relationships and Redeployed

$1.3 Billion in Land Acquisitions and Development Funding Across the Portfolio

Subsequent to Quarter End, Completed $1.1 Billion of Capital Support in Connection with Dream Finders Homes’ Acquisition of Beazer Homes

Generated $0.8 Billion in Net Cash Proceeds from Homesite Sales

 

MIAMI – October 27, 2026 – Millrose Properties, Inc. (NYSE: MRP, “Millrose” or the “Company”), the homesite option platform for residential homebuilders and developers, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Millrose delivered a record third quarter, driven by disciplined underwriting and the resilience and adaptability of our platform in a challenging housing market,” said Darren Richman, Chief Executive Officer and President of Millrose. “We have raised our outlook for the remainder of the year and authorized our first share repurchase program, reflecting our confidence in the durability of our cash flows and our commitment to disciplined capital allocation. With the completion of the Dream Finders financing, approximately 42% of our asset base is now invested outside the Lennar Master Program Agreement, reflecting what Millrose has always been: a comprehensive capital solutions platform for the homebuilding industry. We remain focused on expanding our counterparty relationships, developing innovative financing solutions for homebuilders and creating long-term value for our shareholders.”

 

Financial Highlights

 

Millrose produces recurring cash flow through contractual monthly cash option payments with continuous capital redeployment of homesite sale proceeds.

 

For the third quarter of 2026, Millrose reported:

 

•
Net income attributable to Millrose common shareholders of $130.0 million, or $0.78 per share.
•
Total revenues: $211.0 million from option fees and development loan income.
•
Adjusted Funds From Operations (AFFO): $133.5 million, or $0.80 per share.

 

Total portfolio weighted average annualized yield was 9.3% as of September 30, 2026.

Dividend

 

On September 23, 2026, Millrose declared a quarterly dividend of $131.2 million, or $0.79 per share of Class A and Class B common stock. The dividend was paid on October 15, 2026, to shareholders of record as of October 5, 2026.

 

Share Repurchases

 

In October 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors authorized a new $250 million share repurchase program, reflecting its confidence in the Company’s strong financial performance and long-term prospects, while providing flexibility to opportunistically repurchase shares. Repurchases may be made from time to time in the open market or through privately negotiated transactions, subject to market conditions and other considerations. The program does not obligate the Company to repurchase any specific number of shares and may be suspended or discontinued at any time.

 

Portfolio Highlights

 

•
Lennar Master Program Agreement: The Lennar relationship remains foundational to the Millrose platform, providing a stable base of recurring cash flow. For the third quarter of 2026, Millrose received $596 million in net cash proceeds from homesite sales to Lennar and redeployed $546 million into new land acquisitions and development funding. As of September 30, 2026, the Lennar homesites under option contracts were $6.3 billion and the Lennar Invested Capital balance was approximately $5.9 billion with a weighted average yield of 8.5%.
•
Other Agreements: Millrose funded an additional $714 million under Other Agreements at a weighted average yield of 10.8%, bringing homesites under option contracts and other related assets to $3.8 billion and Invested Capital net of realized homesite sales of $3.4 billion as of September 30, 2026. This capital growth of approximately $521 million compared to the prior quarter reflects the organic expansion of Millrose's business model, including the continued diversification of its builder base to 21 counterparties outside of Lennar.
•
Portfolio Composition: Millrose ended the quarter with 156,729 homesites across 834 communities in 30 states as of September 30, 2026.
•
Industry Consolidation Support: Millrose finalized approximately $1.1 billion capital support in connection with Dream Finders Homes' acquisition of Beazer Homes, effective October 20, 2026, with the acquisition having closed on that date. The arrangement is structured as approximately $580 million (net of option deposits received) in land banking capital across approximately 7,000 homesites, more than half of which are finished, with a weighted average life of approximately 2.5 years, and approximately $488 million (net of original issue discount) in preferred equity in Dream Finders carrying a 12% dividend, of which $171 million was funded on September 14, 2026 and $317 million at closing. Pro forma for the transaction, approximately 42% of Millrose’s asset base is now invested outside the Lennar Master Program Agreement, underscoring the growing role Millrose's platform plays in enabling capital-efficient consolidation across the homebuilding industry.
•
Single-Family Rental Pilot: Millrose acquired an initial portfolio of 1,233 completed single-family homes from Lennar across 18 states for an aggregate cost basis of approximately $333 million, funded with approximately $120 million of balance sheet capital, net of $213 million of limited-recourse secured financing. The acquisition was underwritten as a standalone rental investment,

About Millrose Properties, Inc.

 

Millrose (NYSE: MRP) is the premier homesite option platform for residential homebuilders. The company specializes in the acquisition and horizontal development of land to provide a predictable, just-in-time supply of finished homesites — the most scarce and mission-critical resource in the homebuilding industry. Unlike traditional land bankers, Millrose utilizes a proprietary technology platform that provides real-time feedback and data analytics to drive acquisition decisions. Every transaction in the Millrose portfolio undergoes rigorous independent due diligence to ensure attractive yields and long-term viability. By enabling an asset-light model, Millrose provides its diverse roster of homebuilder and developer partners with the strategic flexibility to maintain production volumes and optimize balance sheet efficiency across all market environments. For more information about Millrose, please visit millroseproperties.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

Invested Capital is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents the balance on which monthly cash option fees are paid by counterparties. Invested Capital includes certain components of our condensed consolidated financial statements related to (i) homesites under option contracts, (ii) development loans receivable, (iii) preferred instruments and (iv) liabilities. The most directly comparable GAAP financial measure is homesites under option contracts as presented in the Company’s condensed consolidated balance sheets. Management uses Invested Capital as a measure of the capital deployed and believes that the figure is useful to investors because it serves as the basis for generating option fees and other related income. This non-GAAP measure is presented solely to permit investors to understand how our management assesses underlying performance and is not, and should not be viewed as, a substitute for GAAP measures, and should be viewed in conjunction with our GAAP financial measures.

 

AFFO means the Adjusted Funds From Operations, which are calculated as the net income (computed in accordance with GAAP), excluding gains (or losses) from sales of property, plus real estate depreciation, adjusted to eliminate the impact of non-recurring items that are not reflective of operations and certain non-cash items that reduce or increase net income (loss) in accordance with GAAP.

 

The Company is unable to provide a reconciliation of quarterly AFFO run rate to the most directly comparable GAAP measure without unreasonable efforts due to the inherent difficulty in forecasting the timing of items that have not yet occurred, as well as quantifying certain amounts that are necessary for such reconciliation.
