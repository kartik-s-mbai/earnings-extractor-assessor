Surf Air Mobility Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results, Meeting Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA Guidance

Third Quarter Revenue of $36.5 Million, Within Guidance, and an Increase of 25% Compared to the Third Quarter 2025, and the Highest in Company’s History

Third Quarter Adjusted EBITDA Loss of $6.7 Million, Within Guidance, and a 32% Improvement Compared to the Third Quarter 2025, and the Best Adjusted EBITDA in Company’s History

Surf On Demand Business Revenue Grew to a Record $19 Million, an increase of 121% Compared to the Third Quarter 2025

Commercially Launched OperatorOS, With Five Contracts Signed to Date

Company Issues Fourth Quarter 2026 Guidance

LOS ANGELES, CA - November 5, 2026 - Surf Air Mobility Inc. (NYSE: SRFM) ("Surf Air Mobility" or the "Company"), a leading air mobility platform, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a progress update for the Company’s SurfOS software, Surf On Demand, and airline operations businesses.

Deanna White, Chief Executive Officer of Surf Air Mobility, said: "The third quarter was the highest revenue and best Adjusted EBITDA quarter in Surf Air Mobility's history, with revenue and Adjusted EBITDA within guidance. We are now in the Expansion Phase of our Transformation Plan, and we made progress on each of its priorities: commercializing SurfOS, scaling Surf On Demand, and expanding airline profitability. These results reflect our shift from an airline-centric company to a technology-powered air mobility platform."

 

Q3 2026 Financial Results

Revenue

●
Total revenue of $36.5 million, within guidance of $35.5 million to $37.5 million, a 25% year-over-year increase and a 24% increase compared to the second quarter of 2026. This was the highest quarterly revenue in Company history.

 

●
Surf On Demand revenue of $19 million, a 121% year-over-year increase and a 57% increase compared to the second quarter of 2026 reflecting increased demand and increased revenue per departure.
●
Airline operations scheduled service revenue of $17.5 million, a 15% year-over-year decrease, primarily reflecting the exit of certain Essential Air Service routes, partially offset by productivity improvements.

 

Net Loss

Net loss was $20.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $27.2 million in the prior year period, a 25% improvement. Net loss included investment in R&D for technology initiatives, stock-based compensation, transaction costs and other non-recurring items.

 

Adjusted EBITDA

●
Adjusted EBITDA loss of $6.7 million, within guidance of $7 million to $4 million, a 32% year-over-year improvement and a 36% improvement compared to the second quarter of 2026.
●
Results reflect growth in profitable revenue in the Surf On Demand business and expanding positive Adjusted EBITDA margins in the airline operations business.

 

Q3 2026 Business Highlights

SurfOS Software

●
Commercially launched OperatorOS, the Company's software for Part 91 and 135 operators, and signed five OperatorOS contracts to date, including three within the third quarter of 2026 and two after quarter end. OperatorOS will earn a percentage of revenue on each trip managed through the software.
●
Received authorization from the FAA for OperatorOS as a system of record for our airline, enabling electronic signatures, records, and manuals.
●
Continued growth of the Powered by Surf On Demand program, which uses BrokerOS.
●
Deployed initial phase of Enterprise BrokerOS at Wheels Up with a contract worth up to $12 million over a three year term.
●
Advanced discussions with an active enterprise pipeline of large operators, brokerages, and aircraft manufacturers that the Company believes is worth tens of millions of dollars in annual revenue.

 

 

Surf On Demand

●
Achieved highest revenue and highest year-over-year revenue growth in a quarter since inception.
●
Accelerated revenue growth every quarter this year, more than doubling during the first nine months of 2026, compared to the same period in 2025.
●
Increased departures to a record 1,417, up 86% year-over-year.
●
Increased average revenue per departure 17% year-over-year and 29% compared to the second quarter of 2026, reflecting a shift toward larger aircraft and longer trips.
●
Improved gross margin more than 4 percentage points year-over-year and more than 10 percentage points compared to the second quarter of 2026.
●
Expanded to seven preferred operator partners in total, providing preferential rates and terms and exclusive access to select aircraft.

 

Airline Operations

●
Generated positive Adjusted EBITDA of $1.2 million for the airline, improving in each quarter of 2026.
●
Achieved SurfOS-enabled operational efficiencies with:
○
Direct operating cost per block hour decreasing 6%
○
Fuel burn decreasing 9%
○
Pilot cost per block hour decreasing 9%
○
Labor productivity increasing 15%, year-over-year
●
Increased Mokulele Airlines revenue 9% year-over-year. Mokulele Airlines now serves 9 airports and 12 routes in Hawaii.
●
Awarded a four-year, $19.4 million Essential Air Service contract to serve Lanai through August 2030.
●
Improved controllable completion factor 2 percentage points to 98%, on-time arrivals increased 4 percentage points to 87% compared to the second quarter of 2026, and on-time departures were up 4 percentage points, to 80%, despite two hurricanes in Hawaii.
●
Joined the FAA’s SMART airspace program as a participating airspace user.

 

Electrification

●
Completed an electric aircraft demonstration program with BETA Technologies, with 64 flights across four Hawaiian islands.

 

●
Became the first Part 135 passenger operator to join the FAA's Center for Advanced Aviation Technologies Consortium.

Corporate

●
Appointed Jason Secore, formerly Chief Financial Officer of Matternet, as Chief Financial Officer.
●
Appointed Barrett Brown, former Head of Asia at Palantir Technologies, as President of SurfOS.

 

Balance Sheet and Financing

●
The second $14 million tranche of the Company's asset-backed loan funded in August, completing the $21.6 million facility.
●
Cash at quarter end was $10.7 million.

 

Jason Secore, Chief Financial Officer of Surf Air Mobility, said: "Our results reflect growth in Surf On Demand's profitable revenue and expanding airline margins. Our priority is to maximize long-term shareholder value by investing in our most profitable and fastest-growing businesses, while continuing to narrow our Adjusted EBITDA loss, improving free cash flow conversion, and funding growth from a stronger balance sheet."

 

Financial Outlook

Surf Air Mobility is providing guidance for the fourth quarter, reaffirming revenue guidance for the full year 2026, and modifying Adjusted EBITDA guidance for the full year 2026:

Fourth Quarter 2026

●
Revenue in the range of $36.4 million to $46.4 million.
●
Adjusted EBITDA loss in the range of $5.4 million to $2.4 million, which excludes the impact of stock-based compensation, changes in fair value of financial instruments, and transaction and restructuring expenses.

Full Year 2026

●
Revenue in the range of $128 million to $138 million.

 

●
Adjusted EBITDA loss in the range of $35 million to $32 million, a modification from $30 million to $25 million. Guidance modification due to higher fuel prices and extended implementation periods for SurfOS.

The Company expects airline Adjusted EBITDA to improve further in the fourth quarter.
