CUMBERLAND PHARMACEUTICALS REPORTS
Q3 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS & COMPANY UPDATE
$100 million Strategic Transaction Closed
Plans to Submit New Drug Application for Ifetroban in DMD
Top-Line Results Announced for Systemic Sclerosis Trial
NASHVILLE, Tenn. (Nov. 10, 2026) – Cumberland Pharmaceuticals Inc. (Nasdaq: CPIX), an innovation-focused biopharmaceutical company developing new products for rare diseases, announced today its third quarter 2026 financial results and provided a company update. Cumberland has announced its decision to submit a New Drug Application (“NDA”) to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (“FDA”) for ifetroban as a potential treatment for the cardiomyopathy associated with Duchenne muscular dystrophy (“DMD”).
The third quarter of 2026 marked Cumberland’s first period of operations following the July 1, 2026, closing of its Strategic Transaction with Apotex Health, the largest pharmaceutical company based in Canada. Cumberland’s commercial brands and organization were integrated into Apotex’s U.S. branded business. The $100 million in proceeds at closing strengthened the company’s balance sheet, simplified its business model and allowed Cumberland to concentrate its resources on its clinical pipeline.
“Our first full quarter as a development-focused company has reinforced the value of concentrating our efforts on our clinical pipeline,” said A.J. Kazimi, CEO of Cumberland Pharmaceuticals. “Our team and resources are now dedicated to developing new medicines for unmet medical needs. We have the financial strength and the expertise to pursue our new mission, as we work to create lasting value for patients and shareholders.”
RECENT COMPANY DEVELOPMENTS INCLUDE:
Plans to File New Drug Application for Ifetroban in DMD
Cumberland announced its decision to submit an NDA to the FDA for ifetroban as a potential treatment for the cardiomyopathy associated with DMD, a rare, fatal genetic neuromuscular disease that results in deterioration of the skeletal, heart and lung muscles. The decision follows a pre-NDA meeting with the FDA this fall. The FDA granted Cumberland’s request for a rolling review, which allows completed sections of the application to be submitted for review before the entire application is filed.
The submission will be supported by data from the Phase 2 FIGHT DMD® trial, which demonstrated a statistically significant 5.4 percentage point improvement in left ventricular ejection fraction (p=0.002) in the high-dose group compared with a control group of placebo-treated patients combined with propensity score-matched natural history patients. Findings also included a reduction in markers of cardiac damage. The NDA submission will also include long-term data from the ongoing open-label extension, preclinical data published in the Journal of the American Heart Association, manufacturing information and a safety database of more than 1,400 subjects across approximately 30 clinical studies.


Through up to 36 months of treatment across the Phase 2 trial and its open-label extension, ifetroban has maintained a favorable safety profile in DMD patients, with no treatment-related serious adverse events or new safety concerns.
There is currently no approved therapy specifically targeting DMD heart disease, the leading cause of death in these patients. Ifetroban has received Orphan Drug, Rare Pediatric Disease and Fast Track designations, and the FDA has conditionally accepted DYSCORBAN® as the proprietary name for the product. Cumberland has also secured a growing portfolio of patents protecting ifetroban for this DMD heart disease indication.
Top-Line Results from Phase 2 Systemic Sclerosis Trial
Cumberland announced top-line results from its Phase 2a ASSIST-SSc trial evaluating ifetroban in patients with Systemic Sclerosis (SSc), a debilitating autoimmune disease characterized by diffuse fibrosis of the skin and internal organs, with no approved therapy for the underlying condition. Ifetroban was generally well tolerated, with a safety profile consistent with prior experience, and no serious adverse event during the 12-month treatment period was assessed as related to study drug.
As next steps, Cumberland plans to submit the clinical study report to the FDA and complete correlative biomarker studies using specimens collected during the trial, along with cardiac imaging evaluations from patients continuing in the open-label extension. These findings will inform the company’s decision on further development of ifetroban in SSc.
New Mission Reflects Focus on Innovation
Following the Strategic Transaction, Cumberland completed a strategic planning process to take a fresh look at who the company is and where it is headed.
The result is a refined mission statement that reflects the company’s emphasis on innovation:
Advancing new therapies that address significant unmet medical needs.
The mission guides Cumberland’s next chapter as an innovation-focused biopharmaceutical company developing new products for rare diseases. It centers the company’s resources and expertise on advancing its clinical-stage pipeline, led by ifetroban, while continuing to build on its experience developing and delivering differentiated medicines for patients.




FINANCIAL RESULTS:
Net Income: During the third quarter of 2026, the revenues and expenses associated with the Strategic Transaction continued to be classified as discontinued operations. Net income for the quarter was $77.8 million, which included the gain on divestiture of the assets associated with the Strategic Transaction, along with the ongoing investment in research and development, supported by the remaining general and administrative expenses.
Balance Sheet: At September 30, 2026, Cumberland had approximately $114 million in total assets, including $80.6 million in cash and cash equivalents. Liabilities totaled $42 million and total shareholders’ equity was $73 million as of September 30, 2026.
