iRhythm Holdings Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

SAN FRANCISCO, November 5, 2026 -- iRhythm Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: IRTC), a leading digital health care company with a mission to create trusted solutions that detect, predict, and prevent disease, today reported financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Revenue of $221.6 million, a 14.9% increase compared to third quarter 2025
•Gross margin of 74.2%, a 310-basis point increase compared to third quarter 2025
•GAAP net loss of $29.9 million, a $24.6 million increase in net loss compared to third quarter 2025
•Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin of $51.2 million and 23.1%, respectively, a $29.6 million and 1,190-basis point improvement, respectively, compared to third quarter 2025
•Unrestricted cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities of $560.7 million as of September 30, 2026
•Increased fiscal year 2026 revenue guidance to $890 million to $895 million and adjusted EBITDA margin guidance to 15.5% to 16.0%

Recent Operational Highlights
•Delivered another strong quarter, driven by healthy volume-led revenue growth of Zio Monitor and robust margin expansion, with broad-based momentum across cardiology, primary care, innovative channels and international markets
•Completed the acquisition of VitalConnect on October 5, 2026, broadening our cardiac monitoring portfolio with an immediate FDA-cleared mobile cardiac telemetry offering, advancing our multi-vitals strategy, and expanding our addressable market across the continuum of care
•Published three large-scale real world studies demonstrating product superiority of Zio AT (CAMELOT-ALERT1), Zio Long-Term Continuous Monitoring (EXCALIBUR2), and improvements in Zio Monitor performance and diagnostic yield compared with Zio XT3 in over 1.7 million total patients
•Submitted FDA 510(k) applications for pediatric use with our Zio Monitor long-term continuous monitoring business
•Closed a new $300 million senior secured revolving credit facility on November 3, 2026, providing additional liquidity to support our long-term growth objectives

“Our third quarter results reflect the continued strength of our core business. Demand for Zio Monitor remains robust, exceeding our expectations in the quarter, driven by volume growth across care settings and channels. We also continued to take meaningful steps forward with significant margin expansion, and consistent execution against our strategic priorities,” said Quentin Blackford, President and Chief Executive Officer of iRhythm. “With the acquisition of VitalConnect now closed, we are encouraged by the early opportunities we see to broaden our capabilities and serve more patients within the ambulatory cardiac monitoring market and across the continuum of care. With an immediate FDA-cleared mobile cardiac telemetry device, we are excited to be able to offer the marketplace a best-in-class MCT portfolio of options. We remain focused on advancing innovation, expanding access to Zio, and investing in the growth initiatives that we believe will create durable value for patients, clinicians, and stockholders.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $221.6 million, up 14.9% from $192.9 million during the same period in 2025. The increase was driven primarily by sustained volume demand across our customer base, reflecting continued strength in our core business and contributions from newer growth channels.

Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was $164.5 million, up 20.0% from $137.1 million during the same period in 2025, while gross margin was 74.2%, a 310-basis point improvement compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase in gross profit was primarily due to increased volume of Zio services, as well as a one-time benefit from tariff refunds of approximately $3.1 million. The increase in gross margin was primarily driven by continued operational efficiencies, product mix, a tariff refund one-time benefit, and disciplined execution.

Operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $197.0 million, compared to $145.6 million for the same period in 2025. Adjusted operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $138.6 million, compared to $141.4 million during the same period in 2025. The decrease in adjusted operating expenses, period over period, reflects a disciplined approach to investment, as we funded key growth initiatives while thoughtfully managing costs across the broader organization.

GAAP net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $29.9 million, or net loss per diluted share of $0.90, compared with GAAP net loss of $5.2 million, or net loss per diluted share of $0.16, for the same period in 2025. Adjusted net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $27.8 million, or net income per diluted share of $0.82, compared with an adjusted net loss of $2.0 million, or net loss per diluted share of $0.06, for the same period in 2025. The improvement was primarily driven by our revenue growth and operating leverage achieved through implementation of efficiency initiatives.

Unrestricted cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $560.7 million as of September 30, 2026.

2026 Annual Guidance
For the full year 2026, iRhythm expects revenue between $890 million and $895 million, which includes approximately $16 million of expected VitalConnect revenue contribution from the October 5, 2026 acquisition close through year-end. iRhythm also expects adjusted EBITDA margin between 15.5% and 16.0%, inclusive of VitalConnect. iRhythm’s guidance reflects continued volume-led growth and operating leverage while maintaining disciplined investment in innovation and market expansion.

About iRhythm Holdings, Inc.
iRhythm is a leading digital health care company with a mission to create trusted solutions that detect, predict, and prevent disease. Combining wearable biosensors and cloud-based data analytics with powerful proprietary algorithms, iRhythm distills data from millions of heartbeats into clinically actionable information. Through a relentless focus on patient care, iRhythm’s vision is to deliver better data, better insights, and better health for all.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
We refer to certain financial measures that are not recognized under U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) in this press release, including adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income (loss), adjusted net income (loss) per diluted share, adjusted operating expenses and free cash flow. We use these non-GAAP financial measures for financial and operational decision-making and as a means to evaluate period-to-period comparisons. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures may be helpful to investors. However, non-GAAP financial measures are presented for supplemental informational purposes only, have limitations as analytical tools, and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with GAAP. In addition, other companies, including companies in our industry, may calculate non-GAAP financial measures differently or may use other measures to evaluate their performance, all of which could reduce the usefulness of our disclosure of non-GAAP financial measures as a tool for comparison. See the schedules attached to this press release for additional information and reconciliations of such non-GAAP financial measures. We have not reconciled our adjusted EBITDA margin estimates for full year 2026 because certain items that impact these figures are uncertain or out of our control and cannot be reasonably predicted. Accordingly, a reconciliation of adjusted EBITDA margin estimates is not available without unreasonable effort.

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) before income tax provision, depreciation and amortization, interest expense, and interest income and as further adjusted for stock-based compensation expense, changes in fair value of strategic investments, impairment charges, business transformation costs, certain intellectual property litigation expenses, certain litigation settlements (net of expected insurance recoveries), costs related to the cybersecurity incident (net of expected insurance recoveries), and loss on extinguishment of debt. Business transformation costs include costs associated with professional services, employee termination and relocation, third-party merger and acquisition, integration, and other costs to augment and restructure the organization, inclusive of both outsourced and offshore resources.
