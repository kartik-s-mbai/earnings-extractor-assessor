EXHIBIT 99.1

Hanmi Reports 2026 Third Quarter Results

LOS ANGELES, Oct. 27, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Hanmi Financial Corporation (NASDAQ: HAFC, or “Hanmi”), the parent company of Hanmi Bank (the “Bank”), today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026.

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $23.7 million, or $0.80 per diluted share, compared with $23.5 million, or $0.79 per diluted share for the second quarter of 2026. The return on average assets for the third quarter was 1.17% and the return on average equity was 10.90%, compared with a return on average assets of 1.20% and a return on average equity of 11.09% for the second quarter of 2026.

CEO Commentary
“Hanmi delivered solid financial performance in the third quarter, driven by 5.8% growth in net interest income and a 10.8% increase in preprovision net revenue,” said Bonnie Lee, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Net interest margin expanded eight basis points to 3.44%, supported by higher yields on interest-earning assets and disciplined funding costs. We also continued to strengthen our deposit base, with total deposits increasing 1.4%, driven primarily by growth in core deposits.”

“In the third quarter, we generated robust loan production of $544 million across our business, reflecting the strength of the Hanmi franchise and healthy market demand. Elevated payoffs partially offset this strong production, resulting in a modest increase in loan balances. More importantly, asset quality remains strong, underpinned by conservative underwriting, proactive portfolio management, and strong credit administration.”

“Looking ahead, our strong capital position, growing deposit base, disciplined expense management, and positive earnings momentum position us well to capitalize on opportunities across our markets and continue delivering long-term value for our shareholders,” concluded Lee.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:

  • Third quarter net income increased 0.8% from the second quarter, with 10.8% growth in preprovision net revenue1 offset by a $3.3 million increase in credit loss expense from a single loan charge-off.
  • Net interest income increased 5.8% to $67.6 million, reflecting a 2.4% growth in average interest-earning assets, which was funded by a 2.8% growth in average deposits.
  • Net interest margin was 3.44%, up eight basis points; the average yield on loans increased 13 basis points while the average rate on interest-bearing deposits increased only one basis point.
  • Deposits grew 1.4% to $7.06 billion, with core deposits up 1.9% and noninterest-bearing deposits stable at 30.4% of total deposits.
  • Loans increased 0.5% to $6.57 billion as new loan production increased 46.2%, or $171.7 million, partially offset by an increase in payoffs of $127.0 million.

  • Hanmi recognized a $3.2 million charge-off on a $21.2 million commercial real estate loan when it was moved to held-for-sale; asset quality, however, remained strong with nonperforming assets to total assets unchanged from the second quarter at 0.12% and a low 0.14% ratio of nonperforming loans to total loans.
  • Hanmi returned 55.6% of third quarter earnings to shareholders, comprising $8.3 million in dividends and $4.9 million in share repurchases; capital ratios remained healthy with tangible common equity to tangible assets1 at 9.93% and a common equity Tier 1 capital ratio of 12.30%.

Results of Operations
Net interest income increased 5.8%, or $3.7 million, to $67.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, from $63.9 million for the second quarter. This increase was principally due to a $3.2 million increase in interest income on loans as well as a $1.5 million increase in interest income from deposits in other banks, partially offset by a $1.5 million increase in interest expense on deposits.

The increase in third quarter net interest income reflected several items. Interest income included $0.4 million of loan interest reversals, $1.2 million of prepayment fees and accelerated deferred fee income from loan payoffs, and a $0.2 million special dividend from FHLB. Interest expense included an additional $0.3 million on subordinated debentures from the overlapping of the newly issued 2036 Notes and the redemption of the 2031 Notes.

Net interest margin (taxable equivalent) increased eight basis points to 3.44% for the third quarter of 2026 from 3.36% for the second quarter. The increase reflects a higher contribution from interest income on bank deposits, partially offset by slightly higher interest expense on customer deposits and subordinated debt. The several items affecting third quarter net interest income noted above represented approximately four basis points of the increase in net interest margin.

The average yield on loans increased 13 basis points to 6.03% for the third quarter of 2026 from 5.90% for the second quarter, while the average rate on interest-bearing deposits increased one basis point to 3.18% for the third quarter from 3.17% for the second quarter. Fees from loan payoffs, partially offset by the reversal of interest income in the third quarter, represented approximately five basis points of the increase in average loan yields.

Average loans increased 0.3%, or $18.0 million, to $6.46 billion for the third quarter of 2026 from $6.44 billion for the second quarter. Average deposits increased 2.8%, or $187.9 million, to $6.94 billion for the third quarter of 2026 from $6.75 billion for the second quarter. Interest-bearing deposits led the increase in average deposits with a $116.3 million increase, primarily due to a $103.4 million increase in money market and savings. The ratio of average loans to average deposits for the third quarter was 93.1%, down from 95.5% for the second quarter. The ratio of average demand deposits to average deposits was 29.3% for the third quarter, essentially unchanged from 29.1% for the second quarter.

Credit loss expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $4.5 million, compared with $1.2 million for the second quarter; the increase resulted from higher net charge-offs. Noninterest income increased 8.2% to $9.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, from $8.3 million for the second quarter of 2026, primarily due to a $1.0 million increase in gain on sales of SBA loans. During the third quarter of 2026, Hanmi Financial Corporation issued $55.0 million of fixed-to-floating subordinated notes due July 31, 2036 (the “2036 Notes”). The 2036 Notes carry a fixed interest rate of 6.50% for the first five years. Thereafter, the 2036 Notes will pay interest at a floating rate, reset quarterly, equal to the then-current three-month Secured Overnight Financing Rate (“SOFR”) plus 234 basis points. The 2036 Notes may be redeemed at the option of Hanmi, without penalty, on July 31, 2031 and any interest payment date thereafter, or earlier upon specified events. The proceeds from the issuance of the 2036 Notes and the proceeds from the sale of securities of $44.5 million were used to redeem the $110.0 million callable subordinated notes due 2031 (the “2031 Notes”).

Management believes the presentation of this financial measure excluding the impact of these items provides useful supplemental information that is essential to a proper understanding of the capital strength of Hanmi.
