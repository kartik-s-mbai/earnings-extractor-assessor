Highlights for the quarter:
•Increased outlook for the full-year 2026, including Same Store NOI by 90bps and Core FFO per share by $0.10, following strong year-to-date performance and reaffirming optimism for the remainder of 2026.
•Closed the acquisitions of National Storage Affiliates and Public Storage Canada, adding over 1,100 stores to the PS Next™ operating platform as part of our PS4.0™ strategy to drive shareholder value.

•Acquired 25 self-storage facilities with 1.7 million net rentable square feet for $258.4 million, excluding the impacts of NSA and PS Canada. Year to date, including activity subsequent to September 30, 2026, the Company acquired or was under contract to acquire 65 self-storage facilities with 4.7 million net rentable square feet, for $713.2 million.

We are moving quickly to integrate these portfolios, deploy the operational advantages of PS Next, and capture the significant value creation opportunities available across our broader North American footprint.”

Investment and Third-Party Management Activity
NSA Acquisition: On July 22, 2026, the Company completed the acquisition of NSA in an all-stock transaction (the "NSA Acquisition"), expanding the Public Storage portfolio to over 4,500 locations and more than 327 million operating square feet.
The NSA Acquisition presents a significant value creation opportunity by applying our industry-leading PS Next revenue and expense management capability to the NSA portfolio. The Company expects to generate approximately $110 million to $130 million of run-rate synergies within the next three to four years, driven by an enhanced customer experience, NOI opportunity, tenant reinsurance uplift, and G&A savings.
PS Canada: On September 1, 2026, the Company completed the acquisition of PS Canada for US $1.2 billion, including assumed debt, with an opportunity for the sellers to receive earn-out consideration of up to 768,000 PS OP Units, contingent on the achievement of certain NOI performance targets. The portfolio consists of 68 properties totaling 5.3 million square feet. The acquisition of PS Canada represents a strategic opportunity to expand the Public Storage platform into major Canadian markets with attractive long-term fundamentals. This portfolio includes high-quality real estate in key markets, carries the Public Storage brand, and offers meaningful upside through our PS Next™ operating platform.
Acquisitions: During the quarter, excluding NSA and PS Canada, the Company acquired 25 self-storage facilities with 1.7 million net rentable square feet for $258.4 million. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, and including activity subsequent to quarter end, the Company acquired or was under contract to acquire 65 facilities with 4.7 million net rentable square feet for $713.2 million.
Developments and Expansions: For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company opened four newly developed facilities and various expansion projects, which together contributed 0.5 million net rentable square feet at a cost of $84.1 million.
At September 30, 2026, the Company had various facilities in development (3.3 million net rentable square feet) estimated to cost $571.2 million and various expansion projects underway (1.3 million net rentable square feet) estimated to cost $217.8 million. Of the $788.9 million of aggregate development and expansion costs, $466.6 million remains to be incurred, primarily over the next 18 to 24 months.
Lending: During the quarter, the Company originated $34.0 million of bridge loan financing for third-party self-storage owners at an average rate of 7.7%. In connection with the NSA acquisition, the Company provided a $237 million mezzanine loan to the PS-NSA JV at an average interest rate of 10.22%. At September 30, 2026, the Company had total notes receivable of $445.3 million at an average annual interest rate of 9.0% and $23.1 million of unfunded loan commitments expected to close in the next twelve months.
Third-Party Management: During the quarter, the Company added a net 31 facilities to its third-party property management program. At September 30, 2026, the Company managed or was under contract to manage 493 facilities (35.4 million net rentable square feet) through the program, including 70 facilities currently under construction.
Capital Markets Activity and Balance Sheet
“We have executed a significant capital markets program in 2026, further strengthening Public Storage’s industry-leading balance sheet while funding substantial external growth,” said Joe Fisher, President and Chief Financial Officer. “Year-to-date, we have completed $6.2 billion of debt capital markets activity and $6.0 billion of common share and partnership unit issuance or committed issuance. This activity has fully funded the acquisitions of NSA and Public Storage Canada, while enhancing our liquidity and financial flexibility and leaving us well positioned to pursue additional value-creating opportunities.”
The Company’s total indebtedness as of September 30, 2026 was $12.2 billion, with $650 million, or 5.3%, maturing in the fourth quarter of 2026. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had approximately $4.1 billion of liquidity through a combination of cash, undrawn capacity on its credit facility, and expected retained cash flow over the next twelve months. During the quarter, the Company completed its inaugural offering in the Canadian markets by issuing C$400 million aggregate principal amount of senior notes at a fixed rate of 4.54% maturing on September 16, 2033. Additionally, the Company issued $900 million aggregate principal amount of senior notes and settled interest rate swaps that reduced the effective interest rate on the issuance to 4.85%. As part of its acquisition of PS Canada the company entered into Canadian dollar cross-currency swaps that reduced the effective interest rate on $900 million of its existing debt from 4.64% to an effective rate of 3.43%. Lastly, through October 26, 2026, the Company has entered into forward sale agreements under its ATM program for a total of 1,230,141 common shares (at a weighted average price of $326.51 per share), which are expected to generate approximately $399 million of future net proceeds upon settlement.

Definitions (unaudited)
Annual contract rent: Represents the agreed-upon monthly rate that is paid by tenants in place at the time of measurement. Contract rates are initially set in the lease agreement upon move-in, and the Company adjusts them from time to time with notice. Contract rent excludes other fees that are charged on a per-item basis, such as late charges and administrative fees, does not reflect the impact of promotional discounts, and does not reflect the impact of rents that are written off as uncollectible.
Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization (“EBITDA”): EBITDA is defined as earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure and is used by us as a supplemental measure of performance. The Company believes investors and analysts utilize EBITDA in a similar manner. EBITDA is not a substitute for net income, operating cash flow, or other related financial measures, in evaluating our operating results. Other REITs may compute this measure differently, so comparisons among REITs may not be helpful.
Funds Available for Distribution (“FAD”): FFO adjusted to exclude certain non-cash charges and to deduct recurring capital expenditures, which do not include capital expenditures for energy efficiencies, including the installation of solar panels, heat pumps, and LED lighting; or capital expenditures for other property enhancements, including acquisition rebrandings and commercial conversions. The Company utilizes FAD in evaluating its ongoing cash flow available for investment, debt repayment, and common distributions. The Company believes investors and analysts utilize FAD in a similar manner. FAD is not a substitute for GAAP net cash flow in evaluating liquidity or ability to pay dividends, because it excludes investing and financing activities presented on our statements of cash flows. In addition, other REITs may compute this measure differently, so comparisons among REITs may not be helpful.
Funds from Operations (“FFO”) and FFO per diluted common share and unit (“FFO per share”): Non-GAAP measures defined by Nareit. The Company believes that FFO and FFO per share are useful to REIT investors and analysts in measuring our performance because Nareit’s definition of FFO excludes items included in net income that do not relate to or are not indicative of our operating and financial performance. FFO represents net income before real estate-related depreciation and amortization, which is excluded because it is based upon historical costs and assumes that building values diminish ratably over time, while the Company believes that real estate values fluctuate due to market conditions. FFO also excludes gains or losses on sale of real estate assets and real estate impairment charges, which are also based upon historical costs and are impacted by historical depreciation. FFO and FFO per share are not a substitute for net income or earnings per share. FFO is not a substitute for net cash flow in evaluating our liquidity or ability to pay dividends, because it excludes investing and financing activities presented on our consolidated statements of cash flows. In addition, other REITs may compute these measures differently, so comparisons among REITs may not be helpful.
The Company also present “Core FFO” and “Core FFO per share and unit” non-GAAP measures that represent FFO and FFO per share excluding the impact of (i) foreign currency exchange gains and losses, (ii) charges related to the redemption of preferred shares, (iii) transaction and integration costs related to the NSA acquisition and PS Canada acquisition, and (iv) certain other non-cash and/or nonrecurring income or expense items primarily representing, with respect to the periods presented below, the impact of corporate transformation costs, loss contingencies, due diligence costs incurred in pursuit of strategic transactions, cancelled project write-off, realized or unrealized gain or loss on private equity investments and non-hedge designated derivative transactions, income tax benefits from certain discrete items, certain CEO transition-related and executive severance costs, and amortization of assumed debt discounts and acquired non real estate-related intangibles, and (v) the income tax effect of each adjustment calculated by applying the tax rate of the applicable tax jurisdiction. The Company reviews Core FFO and Core FFO per share and unit to evaluate our ongoing operating performance, and believes they are used by investors and REIT analysts in a similar manner. However, Core FFO and Core FFO per share and unit are not substitutes for net income and net income per share. Because other REITs may not compute Core FFO or Core FFO per share and unit in the same manner as the Company does, may not use the same terminology, or may not present such measures, Core FFO and Core FFO per share may not be comparable among REITs.
Net operating income (“NOI”): Net operating income or “NOI” is a non-GAAP financial measure that excludes the impact of depreciation and amortization expense, which is based upon historical real estate costs and assumes that building values diminish ratably over time, while the Company believes that real estate values fluctuate due to market conditions. The Company utilizes NOI in determining current property values, evaluating property performance, and evaluating property operating trends. The Company believes that investors and analysts utilize NOI in a similar manner. Direct net operating income (“Direct NOI”), a subtotal within NOI, is a non-GAAP financial measure that excludes the impact of supervisory payroll, centralized management costs, and share-based compensation in addition to depreciation and amortization expense. The Company utilizes direct net operating income in evaluating property performance and in evaluating property operating trends as compared to our competitors. The Company believes that investors and analysts utilize NOI and Direct NOI in a similar manner. These measures are not a

Realized annual rent per occupied square foot: Computed by dividing rental income, before late charges and administrative fees, by the weighted average occupied square feet for the period. This measure excludes late charges and administrative fees in order to provide a better measure of the Company’s ongoing level of revenue. Late charges are dependent upon the level of delinquency, and administrative fees are dependent upon the level of move-ins. In addition, the rates charged for late charges and administrative fees can vary independently from rental rates. This measure takes into consideration promotional discounts, which reduce rental income.
Realized annual rent per available square foot: Computed by dividing rental income, before late charges and administrative fees, by the total available net rentable square feet for the period. Similar to realized annual rent per occupied square foot, this measure excludes late charges and administrative fees, and takes into consideration promotional discounts, which reduce rental income.
Same Store Facilities: Consist of facilities the Company has owned and operated on a stabilized level of occupancy, revenues, and cost of operations since January 1, 2024. The composition of the Company’s Same Store Facilities allows us more effectively to evaluate the ongoing performance of its self-storage portfolio by excluding the impact of fill-up of unstabilized facilities, which can significantly affect operating trends. The Company believes investors and analysts use Same Store Facilities information in a similar manner. However, because other REITs may not compute Same Store Facilities in the same manner as the Company does, may not use the same terminology or may not present such a measure, Same Store Facilities may not be comparable among REITs.
Strategic Acquisitions: A component of acquired facilities that represents the 550 facilities related to the Company’s acquisitions of NSA and PS Canada.

About Public Storage
Public Storage, a member of the S&P 500, is a REIT that primarily acquires, develops, owns, and operates self-storage facilities. At September 30, 2026, we: (i) owned and/or operated 4,764 self-storage facilities with approximately 339 million net rentable square feet located in 42 states, Puerto Rico and Canada and (ii) owned a 35% common equity interest in Shurgard Self Storage Limited (Euronext Brussels: SHUR), which owned 338 self-storage facilities located in seven Western European countries with approximately 19 million net rentable square feet operated under the Shurgard® brand. Our headquarters are located in Frisco, Texas.
